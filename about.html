About This Gallery

A collection of Marvel heroes

This website is a small character gallery featuring heroes from the Marvel Cinematic Universe. It brings together IronMan, Hulk, Thor, Captain Marvel, and SpiderMan in one place.

I chose this theme because these characters have different backgrounds, abilities, and personalities. The cards make it easy to scan the characters and compare their short descriptions.

The site was made with HTML, CSS, and Bootstrap. The home page shows the collection, the details page takes a closer look at IronMan, and the form is a simple example of how a new character could be described.
