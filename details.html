Character Details

IronMan

Role: Team Avengers: Tony Stark

Universe: Marvel Cinematic Universe

A billionaire industrialist, genius inventor, and superhero protecting Earth.

Backstory

Tony Stark built his first armored suit to escape captivity. He later used his inventions to protect people.

Abilities

His suit gives him flight, strength, and repulsor technology. Tony is also a skilled engineer and inventor.

First Appearance

Iron Man (2008)

Personality

Confident, quick-witted, and determined to use his skills to help others.
